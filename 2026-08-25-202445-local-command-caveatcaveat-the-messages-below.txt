  I traced the whole path first. Findings that shape the design:

  - ChannelElevationAssigner.assign rewrites Channel.bedElevations over the same
    spline.points() array that collectPrimitives resampled, and it does not
    resample — so (channelId, knotIndex) stays a valid handle into the fresh
    beds. That is exactly what makes the "don't re-collect" part work.
  - computeRiverGrid (L153) dispatches on instanceof RiverPrimitive, and
    RiverPrimitive is a record (final). So ExtendedRiverPrimitive has to wrap a
    RiverPrimitive and the two carve loops must unwrap it. It must also report
    getType() == RIVER, or comparator sorts it behind the position-only
    primitives and the carve loop stops at index 0.
  - RiverPrimitive's 7-arg constructor derives seed from the elevation.
    Re-pointing a bed through it would reseed the cross-section noise, so the
    bed carve would use a different realization than the shell carve. I'll use
    the 8-arg canonical constructor and carry the original seed through.

  Two consequences of what you asked for, stated once, then I proceed as asked:

  1. PopulateNoiseStep.fineGrainedPrimitivePass already cuts the bed per chunk
     against the tile shell. With the bed now also cut at tile level, that pass
