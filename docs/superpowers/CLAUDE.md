# superpowers/

Dated design and planning artifacts written by the planning skills. Historical record of intent —
the code, not these files, is the authority on what shipped.

## Subdirectories

| Directory | What                                                              | When to read                                                          |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `specs/`  | Design documents and surveys: the problem, the measurements, the chosen shape | Understanding why a change was made the way it was, before re-deciding it |
| `plans/`  | Task-by-task implementation plans derived from a spec              | Tracing which step introduced a behaviour, or executing a plan that has not landed |
